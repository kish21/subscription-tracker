### 👀 Demo — what works after this merges
From the subscriptions table, user clicks "Edit" on a subscription, changes price or billing cycle, and sees dashboard totals recomputed immediately. User clicks "Delete", confirms the alert dialog, and the subscription disappears from the table, upcoming renewals, and totals without full page reload.
